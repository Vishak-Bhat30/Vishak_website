  • Achieved state-of-the-art performance over Tree of Thought, Best of N, Generate-Test, and Chain of Thought on evaluated reasoning tasks.
  • Demonstrated gains on agentic benchmarks (Tau2Bench, Agent Safety Bench, VITA Bench) and non-agentic benchmarks (Maze, Spatial Map, ZebraLogic, VERINA), across 3B to frontier LLMs.
  • Applied interwhen to early stopping, halting reasoning when the model is sufficiently confident to reduce token usage and inference cost.
